# Roadmap

This is an early open-source workshop, not a promise that every image can become a perfect model. Code, documentation and first-party examples are MIT licensed. No hosted service or paid tier is currently offered.

## First: reproducible adoption

- Keep the CLI, templates, repair helpers and export checks reproducible and documented.
- Show targeted before/after repairs using redistributable assets and actual exported-model renders.
- Collect independent installation and modelling experiences through issues. Prefer small reproducible projects with reference permissions and exact versions.
- Run the character/vehicle/prop benchmark against a plain Blender agent and hosted generation plus repair; publish unsuccessful runs too.

## Then: improve demonstrated friction

Potential work, subject to evidence: a Blender interface, additional engine adapters, more stress poses, stronger diagnostics and clearer guided repairs. Do not call adapters production-ready until tested in their target runtime. Provider-independent generation or repair integration needs its own design and tests.

## Optional paid convenience

Managed processing, studio integration, training and support could fund maintenance if users demonstrate demand. Keep the released MIT core usable independently. Pricing, subscriptions, automatic repair guarantees and service availability are not established.
